WORD 01 negotiate UK /nəˈɡəʊ.ʃi.eɪt/ · US /nəˈɡoʊ.ʃi.eɪt/ v. 及物/不及物动词 CET-4 · IELTS 6
to have formal discussions with someone in order to reach an agreement with them
negotiate 谈判；协商；（就价格、条件等）洽谈，商定
搭配negotiate a deal / a contract / a raise；negotiate with sb about/over sth
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
The government has refused to negotiate with the strikers.
政府拒绝与罢工者谈判。
场景例句 · 商务采购
After three rounds of talks, we finally negotiated a 10% discount with the supplier.
三轮磋商之后，我们终于和供应商谈下了 10% 的折扣。
场景例句 · 求职面试
She is negotiating a higher salary before accepting the offer.
她在接受录用前，正在谈一个更高的薪资。
💡 记忆提示拆词根：neg-（不）+ oti（闲暇，同 otiose「闲散的」）→「不得闲」——忙着来回磋商、讨价还价，就是 negotiate。谐音联想：/nəˈɡoʊ/ ≈「你 go」→「你 go 洽谈」→ 谈判。
WORD 02 postpone UK /pəʊstˈpəʊn/ · US /poʊstˈpoʊn/ v. 及物动词 CET-4 · IELTS 6（剑桥标注 B1）
to delay an event and plan or decide that it should happen at a later date or time
postpone 推迟；使延期
搭配postpone a meeting / a trip / a decision；postpone sth until/to + 时间；postpone doing sth
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
They decided to postpone their holiday until next year.
他们决定把假期推迟到明年。
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
The trip has been postponed twice.
这趟行程已经被推迟了两次。
场景例句 · 职场邮件
The Monday review meeting has been postponed to Thursday — please update your calendars.
周一的复盘会已推迟到周四，请大家更新日历。
💡 记忆提示拆词根：post-（之后，同 postwar「战后」）+ pone（放置，同 component「放在一起的零件」）→「往后放」→ 推迟。画面：把日程便利贴从今天这格撕下来，贴到后天那一格。注意搭配是 postpone doing（后接动名词）：We've had to postpone going to France.
WORD 03 reluctant UK & US /rɪˈlʌk.tənt/ adj. 形容词（常接 to do） CET-6 · IELTS 6.5（剑桥标注 C1）
not willing to do something and therefore slow to do it
reluctant 不情愿的；勉强的
搭配be/feel reluctant to do sth；a reluctant yes（勉强的同意）；名词 reluctance，副词 reluctantly
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
I was having such a good time I was reluctant to leave.
我玩得太尽兴了，都不太想走。
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
Many parents feel reluctant to talk openly with their children.
许多家长不太愿意和孩子坦诚交流。
场景例句 · 职场沟通
My manager was reluctant to say no to the client, so we took on another rush project.
经理不好意思对客户说不，于是我们又接下一个赶工的项目。
💡 记忆提示谐音联想：/rɪˈlʌk.tənt/ ≈「驴拉坦克」——画面：一头驴被强行套上缰绳去拉坦克，四蹄蹬地、一百个不情愿 → reluctant。词源上它来自拉丁语 reluctari「奋力抵抗」，同源词 recoil「退缩」。
WORD 04 commute UK & US /kəˈmjuːt/ v. 不及物动词 / n. 可数名词（通勤） CET-4 · IELTS 6
to make the same journey regularly between work and home (v.); the journey between home and work (n.)
commute v. （每天固定路线）上下班往返，通勤；n. 上下班路程
搭配commute to work / between A and B；an hour's commute（一小时通勤）；commuter（通勤者）
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
It's exhausting commuting from Brighton to London every day.
每天从布莱顿通勤到伦敦真让人筋疲力尽。
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
It's at least an hour's commute to work.
上班路上至少要花一个小时。
场景例句 · 城市生活
Since the office moved, I listen to one podcast per commute.
办公室搬了以后，我每趟通勤听一期播客。
💡 记忆提示拆词根：com-（相互）+ mute（变换、交换，同 mutable「可变的」）→ 在两地之间来回「交换」位置 → 通勤。冷知识：commute 还有「减刑」义（commute a sentence），因为本质都是「折算替换」。谐音：/kəˈmjuːt/ ≈「靠 mu特的」→ 靠地铁通勤。
WORD 05 streamline UK & US /ˈstriːm.laɪn/ v. 及物动词 CET-6 · IELTS 6.5–7（商务高频）
to improve an organization's effectiveness, often by simplifying how activities are performed
streamline 精简（流程），使更有效率；使成流线型
搭配streamline a process / operations / the workflow；streamlined adj.（精简高效的）
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
Leading organizations use software to streamline their workflow.
领先的组织会借助软件来精简工作流程。
来源：Cambridge Dictionary
Streamlining cars increases their fuel efficiency.
把汽车设计成流线型能提升燃油效率。
场景例句 · 流程优化
We streamlined the approval process from five signatures down to two.
我们把审批流程从五个签核环节精简到两个。
💡 记忆提示拆合成词：stream（流）+ line（线）→「流线」。画面：鲨鱼和高铁都是流线型，阻力最小、速度最快；把臃肿的流程也「削」成一条顺滑流线，就是 streamline（精简提效）。写作里替换 make ... more efficient，很加分。
